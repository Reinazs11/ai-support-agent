Billing Policy

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the first day of each month.

If a card payment fails, support should ask the customer to update the card and
the system should retry the payment after 24 hours and again after 72 hours.

Billing contact changes require approval from the account owner.

Customers must update their tax ID before the invoice is issued. After an
invoice is issued, tax ID corrections require a billing support ticket.
